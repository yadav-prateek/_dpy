ABOUT ME
---

Welcome to my academic and technical blog! I am an educator, teacher, researcher, and technologist passionate about the intersection of networking, edge computing, and artificial intelligence.

I am using this platform to break down complex topics in modern network architecture, share open-source code snippets in Python and C, and document my ongoing research in fog-based content delivery.  

### What You Will Find Here

* ✔ **Deep dives** into Content Distribution Networks (CDN) and edge/fog node optimization
* ✔ **Tutorials and insights** on using tools like Cisco Packet Tracer, LaTeX, MATLAB, CPLEX, and Linux
* ✔ **Discussions on cryptography**, drawn from my background analyzing Salsa and ChaCha ciphers 
* ✔ **Thoughts on teaching**, mentorship, and building technology with integrity and courage

---

### About Myself

I currently serve as an Assistant Professor at Bennett University's School of Artificial Intelligence, Greater Noida, India. I recently completed my PhD at IIT Delhi and have prior experience as a Visiting Faculty member at JNU Delhi and MNNIT Allahabad, and as a researcher at SAG DRDO. I am driven by curiosity and the belief that knowledge must bring the best change in someone's life and, most importantly, a well-rounded and responsible citizen.  

🚀 **Let’s learn, research, and build together!**
